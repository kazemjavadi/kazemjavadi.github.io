NoSQL Distilled

Posted on April 24, 2025 | In: NotSQL

NoSQL databases have become a critical tool for modern applications that require scalability, performance, and flexibility in data storage. Unlike traditional relational databases, NoSQL databases offer schema-less structures, horizontal scaling, and high availability.

In this post, we explore the differences between document stores, key-value pairs, column-family databases, and graph databases. Each of these types is suited to different kinds of problems and data models.

We’ll also discuss when it makes sense to move away from SQL and embrace a NoSQL solution, and what trade-offs to consider in terms of consistency, availability, and partition tolerance (CAP Theorem).

Stay tuned for more deep dives into specific NoSQL technologies and real-world use cases!
